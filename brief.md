## Overview 
A national Single Sign-On (SSO) service allows citizens to use one account to access different government websites and mobile applications. Although this makes access easier, the authority must ensure that citizens' accounts and personal information remain secure. Software standards help verify users' identities and control access to government services. Artificial Intelligence (AI) can help identify unusual login attempts, but its results must be checked to reduce errors and protect privacy. Mobile security is also important because applications should request only the permissions they need. Networking technologies help protect information as it travels between users and servers, while cybersecurity measures reduce risks such as phishing and stolen passwords. Data protection ensures that citizens' information is collected and handled responsibly under Oman's laws. These fields work together, as a weakness in one area may affect the entire service. This brief focuses on Domain Name System over HTTPS (DNS over HTTPS or DoH), explaining how it protects DNS queries, its limitations, and why additional security measures are still necessary.
